Relative finder

Closest reference relatives of an unknown DNA fragment and an identity hypothesis for expert review. It is never a confirmed identification. Support = share of relatives that agree; held-out accuracy = how often the same rule was right when a known species was hidden from the reference. Diet, chromosomes and hormones cannot be read from DNA in water: they count as evidence only when measured on a specimen and entered below.
